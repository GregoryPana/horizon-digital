## Design Context

### Users
Small business owners in Seychelles — guesthouse, restaurant, boutique, tour operator, wellness studio operators. Primarily sole decision-makers. Browse on mobile phones, often on moderate connections. Non-technical, price-conscious, time-poor. They've likely been burned by vague agency promises before. Core anxieties: trust, cost transparency, local relevance, "will it actually bring me customers?"

### Brand Personality
**Tropical Precision** — three words: *cinematic*, *direct*, *local*.

This is a one-person studio operating at boutique quality. The aesthetic is deliberately anti-tropical: dark, refined, purposeful. The contrast between the dark cinematic presentation and the warm copy voice is intentional — this is not a template shop. The brief's own words: "If this website were a person: they'd wear well-tailored all-black with one statement piece."

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Dark-mode only. No toggle. Non-negotiable brand decision.
- **Background**: Near-black `#0D0D0F` — Zinc-950 depth, not flat black
- **Primary accent**: Muted cyan-blue `#5ED1DE` — used *sparingly*. The brand rule: cyan is for primary CTAs and max one emphasis phrase per section. The cyan-on-dark aesthetic must be executed with restraint, NOT as a neon/glow parade
- **Supporting accent**: Deep teal `#14B8A6` for nav and structural UI
- **Typography**: Switzer (body) + Satoshi (display/headings). Both self-hosted woff2. These are the right choices for this brand — preserve them
- **Animation intent**: Purposeful, not decorative. WebGL/shader hero, Framer Motion scroll reveals, GSAP ScrollTrigger. Spring physics preferred over linear easing
- **Anti-references**: Generic bright tropical Seychelles imagery, template-style layouts, "AI purple-blue gradient" palette

### Design Principles
1. **Restraint over spectacle** — Every cyan glow must earn its place. One accent emphasis per section maximum. The brand power comes from what is *not* glowing
2. **Trust through specificity** — Content choices (SCR pricing, live client URLs, local positioning) must never be undermined by generic visual patterns
3. **Mobile-first in reality, not just code** — The target audience is primarily mobile. Any interaction pattern that works beautifully on desktop but awkwardly on phones is a conversion failure
4. **Copy-first hierarchy** — The message is the design. Typography and layout serve the words, not the other way around
5. **Dark excellence, not dark default** — This dark theme must feel *chosen* and *considered*, not defaulted to. Every surface should feel intentional

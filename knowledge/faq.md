# Questions We Hear Most

**How much does a website cost?**
Foundation from SCR 7,500 · Starter from SCR 12,500 · Growth from SCR 25,000 · Custom priced per scope. All pricing is transparent with no hidden fees.

**What's the difference between packages?**
Mainly the number of pages, level of design customisation, revision rounds, and length of post-launch support. Starter and Growth also include more advanced SEO and analytics setup.

**Can I start small and expand later?**
Absolutely. We can launch with a focused scope and extend in phases as your business grows.

**How long does it take?**
Most projects launch in 3 to 6 weeks. We'll give you a clear estimate before we start.

**How many revisions are included?**
Foundation: 1 round. Starter and Growth: 2 rounds. Additional rounds can be scoped separately.

**Do I own the website?**
Yes. Once the project closes, the final codebase and all approved assets are yours. Always.

**Will it work on mobile?**
Yes — we build mobile-first. Your site will look great and work perfectly on any device.

**Can I edit the content myself?**
We build high-performance custom-coded sites, not CMS platforms like WordPress or Wix. This means better speed and security. We handle content updates for you so your site stays fast and looking exactly as intended.

**Who controls my domain?**
Your domain stays in your registrar account. We can assist with setup and renewals, but ownership is always yours.

**What is hosting?**
Hosting keeps your site live online 24/7. You can host with us (SCR 2,500/year — we manage everything) or choose your own provider.

**Is support included after launch?**
Yes. Foundation: 30 days · Starter: 45 days · Growth: 60 days — all after launch.

**Is pricing flexible?**
Package pricing gives a clear starting range, then we adjust based on page count, content complexity, and any extra functionality.

**Do you work with businesses that already have a website?**
Yes. We can redesign your current site or rebuild it on a cleaner structure if performance and content flow need major improvements.

# A Process You'll Actually Enjoy

No surprises. Clear timelines. Defined milestones.

## Step 1 — Discovery & Goals
We learn about your business, your customers, and what you need the website to do. No jargon — just a clear plan that fits your goals. You'll always know what's coming next.

## Step 2 — Design & UX
We shape the layout, look, and structure of your site. You review designs before we write a single line of code, so the result is exactly what you envisioned.

## Step 3 — Build & Test
Once you approve the designs, we build and test across all devices. No radio silence — regular updates throughout.

## Step 4 — Launch & Optimise
We launch when every detail looks right, then stay available during your support period to keep things running smoothly.

## Timelines
Most projects launch in **3 to 6 weeks**, depending on scope and how quickly you can share content and feedback.

## How involved do you need to be?
We keep the process lightweight. You mainly review milestone drafts, approve direction, and share your business details. We handle the rest.

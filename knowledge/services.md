# Our Services

## What we build
We design and build websites for Seychelles businesses — from guesthouses and restaurants to retail shops and professional services. Our goal is always the same: help you receive more enquiries, bookings, and connections from people already looking for what you offer.

## Custom Website Build
We handle everything from planning and custom coding to the final launch. We don't use templates — we build sites around your specific services and customers so the result feels authentic to your brand.

## Design Refresh
If your current website looks dated or isn't performing, we can modernise it without losing what already works. We improve speed, the mobile experience, and the path customers take to contact you.

## Search Visibility Setup
Every site comes with a solid search foundation. Google can easily understand what you offer, making it easier for local customers to find you when they search.

## Built-in Connection Tools
We include the tools your customers actually use — secure enquiry forms and direct WhatsApp integration — so you can start conversations with new leads immediately.

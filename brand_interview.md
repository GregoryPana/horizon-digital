# Brand Discovery Interview: Horizon Digital

## 1. What is the name of the business that will be using this cloned design?

**Business Name:** Horizon Digital
**Tagline:** Empowering Your Digital Horizon

Horizon Digital is a boutique web design and development studio that prioritizes structure, growth, and high-performance digital foundations. The name "Horizon" signifies the future-oriented, scalable, and expansive nature of the solutions provided, while "Digital" anchors the business in the modern tech landscape.

---

## 2. What does the business do? (e.g., Are you a creative agency, a freelancer, or a different type of service provider?)

**Business Model:** Boutique Web Design & Development Studio / Digital Growth Partner.

Horizon Digital functions as a specialized service provider that bridges the gap between simple template builders and high-end, luxury-only creative agencies. We are a **performance-first digital studio** focused on:

- **Custom Website Design & Development:** Building unique, responsive, and fast-loading sites from scratch using modern stacks (React, TypeScript, Tailwind).
- **Design Refreshes:** Modernizing existing digital presences to align with current industry standards and user expectations.
- **Infrastructure & Growth:** Providing structured web solutions that aren't just aesthetically pleasing but are built to scale as the client's business grows.
- **Conversion Strategy:** Crafting layouts specifically designed to turn visitors into leads and customers.

---

## 3. What is the core problem you're solving for your clients?

**The Problem:** Growing businesses often find themselves trapped between "cheap but broken" DIY templates and "overpriced/over-engineered" luxury agency solutions. They lack a professional, credible digital home that actually drives business results.

**The Horizon Solution:**

- **Establishing Immediate Credibility:** We solve the "trust gap" by delivering a premium, tech-forward aesthetic that makes small businesses look like industry leaders.
- **Eliminating Technical Friction:** Many clients struggle with slow, non-responsive, or hard-to-manage websites. We provide high-performance, mobile-optimized infrastructure that is stable and fast.
- **Bridging the Lead Generation Gap:** We move beyond "brochure-ware" to create websites that act as active sales tools, integrating search-ready setups and conversion-focused calls to action (CTAs).
- **Scalability Insurance:** We build on modern frameworks that allow businesses to expand their digital footprint without needing to rebuild from scratch every two years.

---

## 4. Who is your target audience? (Describe the person visiting the site.)

**Primary Audience Strategy:** The "Serious Small Business" & "Growing SME."

**The Visitor Profile:**

- **The Boutique Owner:** Owners of guesthouses, small hotels, or unique local businesses (Seychelles-centric but globally minded) who have a great physical product but a lagging digital presence.
- **The Service Professional:** Consultants, clinic owners, or tradespeople who are already generating revenue and now need their website to reflect their professional success.
- **The Modern SME Manager:** Decision-makers at growing companies who value design and performance but are pragmatic about budget and ROI.

**User Psychographics:**

- Values **clarity and professionalism** over "flashy" or "playful" distractions.
- Is looking for a **reliable partner**, not just a one-off vendor.
- Desires a **clean, structured, and premium** feel that implies technical competence.
- Is often busy and needs information presented directly with **no fluff or hype**.

---

## 5. Do you have an existing logo or brand colors?

**Yes, a highly defined visual identity system is already in place.**

### **Logo Identity:**

- **Iconography:** A geometric capital "H" consisting of two vertical angular pillars.
- **Signature Motif:** A glowing horizontal teal "horizon line" that cuts through the center of the "H," representing growth, expansion, and future direction.
- **Typography:**
  - **"HORIZON"**: Bold white sans-serif.
  - **"DIGITAL"**: High-contrast teal.

### **The "Abyss" Color Palette (Dark-First Premium):**

- **Background Base:** `#0A0A0C` (Deep near-black grey)
- **Primary Accent:** `#00E5FF` (Cyan Glow) – Used for action, certainty, and primary highlights.
- **Secondary Accent:** `#22F1D6` (Teal) – Supporting energy, used in the "horizon line."
- **Primary Text:** `#F2F4F7` (Off-white / light grey) – For high-readability headers.
- **Secondary/Muted Text:** `#8E9196` – For descriptions and long-form body copy.
- **Borders/Dividers:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` (Thin, sharp, disciplined).

### **Visual Language / Mood:**

- **Atmosphere:** Dark, cinematic, and technical.
- **Glassmorphism:** Subtle use of blur and transparency on elevated panels (`bg-white/5` + `backdrop-blur`).
- **Typography Mix:** `Poppins` for bold, high-impact headings; `Switzer` for clean, professional body text.
- **Motion:** Smooth, deliberate reveals using `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` to maintain a premium, structured feel.

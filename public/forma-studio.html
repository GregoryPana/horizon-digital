The Studio

A practice built on
quiet conviction.

Forma Studio was founded in 2009 by Luca Ferrara and Elise Hartmann — two architects who shared a single conviction: that the most enduring spaces are those that hold their restraint. Fourteen years on, that conviction has shaped over sixty completed projects across Europe.

We are a practice of eight. Small enough that every project receives its principal's full attention. Large enough to carry complex commissions from conception to completion without diminishing the idea that started them.

How we work

From first conversation
to final detail.

01
Discovery

An unhurried conversation about how you live, what matters to you, and what the space is for.

02
Concept

Spatial and material proposals. We present one strong direction — not three hedged options.

03
Development

Technical drawings, material boards, and full specification. Every decision documented before a single wall is touched.

04
Construction

On-site oversight and contractor coordination throughout. The design doesn't end at handover — it begins there.

05
Handover

A final walkthrough. A maintenance guide. And an open door — we remain available to every client, indefinitely.

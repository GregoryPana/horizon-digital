# Answers to the 5 Critical Website Questions

**Business Name:** Horizon Digital
**Project:** Website Redesign & Brand Strategy

---

## 1. Who is this website for?

**The Visitor:** Ambitious "Serious Small Business" owners and "Growing SME" managers.

- **Primary Persona:** A business owner who has a successful physical product or service (e.g., a boutique guesthouse in the Seychelles, a professional medical clinic, or a specialized consultancy) but whose current digital presence is either non-existent or "cheap/broken."
- **What they care about:** They care about **credibility, professionalism, and ROI**. They are busy people who have no time for fluff or hype. They want to know that their website will be a reliable, high-performance tool that helps them scale, not a liability they have to worry about.

---

## 2. What's the ONE action you want them to take?

**Primary CTA:** **Book a Free Consultation.**

- **Rationale:** As a boutique studio, the high-touch "Discovery & Scope" phase is critical. The website’s primary success metric is the number of qualified leads who book a discovery call to discuss their specific digital needs.
- **Button Text:** "Book a Free Consult" or "Start Your Redesign."

---

## 3. What objections does the visitor have?

| Potential Objection                         | How the Website Addresses It                                                                                                                                                    |
| :------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **"Can I trust this studio?"**              | By showcasing a **Premium, Technical Aesthetic** ("Abyss" theme) and professional "Work" case studies that prove we are more than just a template-pusher.                       |
| **"Will it be too complex/expensive?"**     | By positioning as a **"Boutique Growth Partner"** rather than a luxury-only agency, offering structured packages (Foundation, Starter, Growth) that imply pragmatism and value. |
| **"Will the site be slow or hard to use?"** | By explicitly highlighting **"Performance-First"** development (React/TS/Tailwind), mobile optimization, and high Lighthouse scores.                                            |
| **"Is this right for my niche?"**           | By including **Industry-Specific Tags** and sections tailored to guesthouses, cafes, and service professionals.                                                                 |

---

## 4. What's the vibe?

**The Vibe:** **Premium Technical.**

- **Visual Direction:** If this website was a person, they would be a **high-end technical architect**. They dress in dark, tailored charcoal, value precision above all else, and speak with quiet confidence.
- **Aesthetic Details:** Dark-first UI, subtle glassmorphism for structure, cinematic teal/cyan glows for highlights, and a focus on "Disciplined Whitespace."
- **Keywords:** Modern, Structured, Confident, Clean, Growth-focused.

---

## 5. Do you have existing brand assets?

**Yes. We are building upon a highly defined identity.**

- **Logo:** A geometric capital "H" with vertical angular pillars and a glowing horizontal teal "horizon line."
- **Colors (The "Abyss" Palette):**
  - **Background:** `#0A0A0C` (Deepest Charcoal)
  - **Primary Accent:** `#00E5FF` (Cyan Glow)
  - **Secondary Accent:** `#22F1D6` (Horizon Teal)
- **Fonts:**
  - **Headings:** `Poppins` (Bold & Structured)
  - **Body:** `Switzer` (Clean & Professional)
- **Design Rules:** Subtle `backdrop-blur`, sharp 8% white borders, and architectural reveals.

---

## Summary Relume Prompt

> **Company:** Horizon Digital, a performance-first boutique web design studio for growing SMEs and boutique hospitality owners.
> **Goal:** Generate consultation leads through a "Book a Free Consult" CTA.
> **Notes:** Premium Technical vibe, Abyss dark theme (Background: #0A0A0C, Accents: #00E5FF, #22F1D6). Focus on credibility, scalability, and performance. Mention 5-step process: Discovery, Content, Design, Build, Launch.

Anse Takamaka · South Mahé
The Location

South Mahé's
hidden quarter.

Anse Takamaka sits on the quieter, wilder south coast of Mahé — away from the resort strip, facing open Indian Ocean. The beach takes its name from the takamaka tree whose enormous roots hold the granite boulders in place at the water's edge.

Victoria, the capital, is a 35-minute drive through the Morne Seychellois National Park — a journey that passes vanilla plantations, cinnamon forests, and the island's only rum distillery, La Plaine St. André.
